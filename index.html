Undertale Port Fan-Project

An unofficial, fan-made HTML5 port of Undertale.

First-time setup. Choose the game files once. They are stored in this browser and loaded automatically from then on.

On phones, use "Select files" and pick data.win plus the mus_*.ogg files (music is optional). Folder selection is not supported on iOS.

Keys: arrows move, Z/Enter confirm, X/Shift cancel, C/Ctrl menu, F4 fullscreen. Saves live in this browser.
